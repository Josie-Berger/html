Summer in Hawaii

My favorite places and things from Oahu

My Favorite Things to do on Oahu!

Waimea Beach

  • Sometimes the dolphins come into the bay, and you can see them jump.
  • You can jump off the rock there known as “Da Big Rock.”
  • The beach gets busy quickly, and parking is limited, so it is important to get there early.

Snorkeling in Shark’s Cove

  • Shark’s Cove has lots of beautiful fish to see.
  • Sometimes you can view sea turtles.
  • There are a lot of sea urchins in the rocks, so watch out!

Hiking

Some of my favorite hikes include:

  • Crouching Lion
  • Manoa Falls
  • ‘Ehukai Pillbox Trail
  • Koko Head

Shelling

Searching for shells at different beaches. Some of the best beaches are:

  • Sunset Beach
  • Ke Iki Beach

Shelling is best in the early morning or after a big swell.

Sunsets

  • Sunsets are best on the North Shore.
  • Sunset Beach is known for its amazing sunsets.
  • ‘Ehukai Pillbox gives a great view at sunset.
